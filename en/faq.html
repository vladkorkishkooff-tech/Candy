Timing · payment · support

FAQ

Short answers about timing, payment, start and post-launch support.

01How long does a project take?

A Telegram bot usually takes 3–10 days. A small website often takes 5–10 working days. A turnkey launch takes 1–4 weeks.

02What if I do not have a brief?

We will assemble it together. The quiz on the main page helps collect the task, features, timeline, materials and budget.

03How does payment work?

Usually the project is split into stages: initial prepayment, approval of the key result, and final payment before launch.

04Do you make revisions after delivery?

Yes. After launch there is a support period to adjust details and make sure the flows work reliably.

05What do you need from me to start?

Service description, contacts, brand materials, examples, access to needed services and the desired result.

06Can you work with an existing website or bot?

Yes, an existing website, Telegram bot, quiz or automation can be improved if access and the task are clear.

07What does post-launch support include?

Critical issue review, consultations on flows and small adjustments within the agreement.

08Do you provide a guarantee?

Yes, the guarantee covers fixing errors in the implemented logic after launch if the flow is used as agreed.
